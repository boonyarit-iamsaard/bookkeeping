The owner reports that the first save felt nearly instant, produced no

This corrects the earlier transcription of the volume charge as $0.0367 and
the resulting incorrect total of $0.1892. Railway rounds current usage to

for the current billing period.

The workspace is on Hobby with $5 of included monthly usage. Railway's
[pricing documentation](https://docs.railway.com/pricing/plans), checked on
2026-10-04, specifies a $5 monthly subscription including $5 of resource usage;
usage below that allowance incurs no additional resource charge. The displayed
$0 usage bill therefore does not mean hosting is free. The expected standard
monthly total is about $5 before any taxes or account-specific adjustments,
within the $10 budget, based on the current estimate. This is a first-week
review, not a guarantee of future usage.
